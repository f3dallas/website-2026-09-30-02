Fitness · Fellowship · Faith

WHAT IS F3?

Free outdoor workouts. Men who show up for one another. A chance to grow as a leader in your family and community.

Mission

“To plant, grow, and serve small workout groups for men for the invigoration of male community leadership.”

The foundation

FIVE CORE PRINCIPLES

  1. 01

    Free of charge

    No dues, fees, or membership requirements.

  2. 02

    Open to all men

    All fitness levels and physical abilities are welcome.

  3. 03

    Held outdoors

    Rain or shine, hot or cold.

  4. 04

    Peer-led

    Participating members rotate responsibility for leading workouts.

  5. 05

    Ends with a Circle of Trust

    Every workout concludes together.

Philosophy

“Leave no man behind,
but leave no man where you find him.”

You belong here

NEW TO F3

Your first workout starts with showing up. You do not need a certain fitness level, special gear, or a friend in the group. Tell us you are new, and we will help you get started.

Tips for your first workout

  • No registration is required.
  • Pick a workout and show up.
  • Arrive a few minutes early and introduce yourself.
  • Wear normal workout clothes and athletic shoes.
  • Bring water if desired.
  • All fitness levels are welcome.
  • Modify exercises as needed.
  • Workouts are free and outdoors.

Frequently asked questions

F3 stands for Fitness, Fellowship, and Faith. It is a network of free outdoor workouts for men, led by the men who attend. Fitness brings us together; fellowship builds friendships; faith means looking beyond yourself and serving others. F3 Dallas is part of F3 Nation, and men of every faith—or no faith—are welcome.

Free peer-led outdoor workouts for men across Dallas.
